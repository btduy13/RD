## Q1. Thuế TNCN 2026: schedule, family deduction, withholding, declaration, journal entries

### Takeaway
For resident salary income, the 2026 tax year uses the new 5-bracket schedule of Luật Thuế TNCN 2025 (No. 109/2025/QH15), from 01/01/2026. The new family deductions also apply: 15.5 million/month for the taxpayer, 6.2 million/month per dependant. The Law takes effect 01/7/2026, but Art. 29(2) makes the salary rules apply to the whole 2026 tax period. The guiding decree is Nghị định 253/2026/NĐ-CP, effective 01/7/2026. It raises the 10% casual-income withholding threshold from 2 million to 5 million per payment. Between 15/4/2026 and 28/02/2027, salary PIT returns (05/KK-TNCN) must be filed quarterly.

### Cited Findings
**Law and effective dates**
- Luật Thuế TNCN No. 109/2025/QH15 is dated 10/12/2025. Art. 29(1): effective 01/7/2026. Art. 29(2): "Các quy định liên quan đến thu nhập từ kinh doanh, từ tiền lương, tiền công của cá nhân cư trú áp dụng từ kỳ tính thuế năm 2026." Art. 29(3): the old Law 04/2007/QH12 and its amendments lapse. For salary and business income they lapse from tax period 2026. — [baochinhphu.vn (Thuế TP.HCM reply quoting the Law)](https://baochinhphu.vn/thoi-diem-ap-dung-bieu-thue-thu-nhap-ca-nhan-moi-102260324150030818.htm)
- Luật 109/2025/QH15 was later amended by Luật số 09/2026/QH16. The National Assembly passed it on 24/4/2026, effective on signing. It amends the PIT, VAT, CIT and SCT laws on income management, tax declaration, finalization and taxpayer duties. The fetched article did not give the specific article changes. — [LuatVietnam](https://luatvietnam.vn/tin-van-ban-moi/da-co-luat-so-09-2026-qh16-sua-doi-cac-luat-thue-tncn-thue-gtgt-thue-tndn-thue-ttdb-186-109001-article.html); [EasyInvoice notes ND 253 guides "Luật 109/2025/QH15 (được sửa đổi, bổ sung bởi Luật số 09/2026/QH16)"](https://easyinvoice.vn/nghi-dinh-253-2026-nd-cp/)

**Progressive schedule (Art. 9, Luật 109/2025/QH15), 5 brackets**

| Bậc | Taxable income/month (million VND) | Taxable income/year (million VND) | Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | up to 10 | up to 120 | 5% |
| 2 | over 10 to 30 | over 120 to 360 | 10% |
| 3 | over 30 to 60 | over 360 to 720 | 20% |
| 4 | over 60 to 100 | over 720 to 1,200 | 30% |
| 5 | over 100 | over 1,200 | 35% |

— [baochinhphu.vn (Art. 9 table reproduced)](https://baochinhphu.vn/thoi-diem-ap-dung-bieu-thue-thu-nhap-ca-nhan-moi-102260324150030818.htm); [VnExpress: from 7 brackets to 5, first bracket widened to 10 million/month](https://vnexpress.net/7-diem-moi-nguoi-lao-dong-can-luu-y-khi-tinh-thue-tncn-nam-2026-5093991.html)
- Tax base (Art. 8(2)): total taxable income, minus compulsory BHXH/BHYT/BHTN contributions, minus voluntary pension and life insurance up to the Government cap, minus the deductions in Art. 10 and Art. 11. — [baochinhphu.vn](https://baochinhphu.vn/thoi-diem-ap-dung-bieu-thue-thu-nhap-ca-nhan-moi-102260324150030818.htm)
- Timing (Art. 8(3)): salary income is recognised when it is paid or received. MoF confirmed that December 2025 salary paid in January 2026 falls in tax period 2026. It therefore gets the new deductions. — [Tạp chí Kinh tế - Tài chính (MoF)](https://tapchikinhtetaichinh.vn/bo-tai-chinh-huong-dan-ap-dung-giam-tru-gia-canh-trong-giai-doan-chuyen-tiep-146548.html)
- Amounts withheld under the old rules from 01/01/2026 to before 01/7/2026 need no re-filed monthly or quarterly returns. Employers adjust them in the 2026 annual finalization. — [EasyInvoice summary of ND 253](https://easyinvoice.vn/nghi-dinh-253-2026-nd-cp/)

**Family deduction (giảm trừ gia cảnh)**
- Taxpayer: 15.5 million/month (186 million/year). Dependant: 6.2 million/month. Old amounts were 11 million and 4.4 million under NQ 954/2020/UBTVQH14. Both Art. 10(1) of Luật 109/2025/QH15 and Nghị quyết 110/2025/UBTVQH15 set these amounts. NQ 110 is effective 01/01/2026 and applies to tax period 2026. — [Tạp chí Kinh tế - Tài chính (MoF)](https://tapchikinhtetaichinh.vn/bo-tai-chinh-huong-dan-ap-dung-giam-tru-gia-canh-trong-giai-doan-chuyen-tiep-146548.html); [LuatVietnam (Art. 1 NQ 110/2025)](https://luatvietnam.vn/thue-phi-le-phi/cach-tinh-thue-tncn-2026-565-106276-article.html)
- A dependant now qualifies with average income up to 3 million/month from all sources. The old limit was 1 million. — [VnExpress](https://vnexpress.net/7-diem-moi-nguoi-lao-dong-can-luu-y-khi-tinh-thue-tncn-nam-2026-5093991.html)
- New deductions for medical and education costs (ND 253, Art. 49(2)): medical costs within the BHYT list up to 23 million/year, and domestic tuition up to 24 million/year. Each needs invoices in the name of the taxpayer or dependant. — [LuatVietnam](https://luatvietnam.vn/thue-phi-le-phi/diem-moi-cua-nghi-dinh-253-2026-nd-cp-ve-thue-thu-nhap-ca-nhan-565-110102-article.html)
- Voluntary pension and life insurance deduction cap: 3 million/month (was 1 million). This covers employer-paid and employee-paid amounts (ND 253 Art. 46(2)(a)). — [LuatVietnam](https://luatvietnam.vn/thue-phi-le-phi/diem-moi-cua-nghi-dinh-253-2026-nd-cp-ve-thue-thu-nhap-ca-nhan-565-110102-article.html)

**Guiding decree: Nghị định 253/2026/NĐ-CP**
- Dated 30/6/2026, effective 01/7/2026. It replaces ND 65/2013/NĐ-CP and parts of ND 91/2014 and ND 12/2015. It has 7 chapters and 71 articles. Its salary and business-income provisions apply to the whole 2026 tax period. — [EasyInvoice](https://easyinvoice.vn/nghi-dinh-253-2026-nd-cp/); [Thuế Phú Thọ via Tạp chí KT-TC](https://thuehaiquan.tapchikinhtetaichinh.vn/huong-dan-khau-tru-10-thue-tncn-voi-khoan-chi-tu-5-trieu-dong-lan-168499.html)
- Thông tư 87/2026/TT-BTC guides the Law and ND 253. — [VnExpress](https://vnexpress.net/7-diem-moi-nguoi-lao-dong-can-luu-y-khi-tinh-thue-tncn-nam-2026-5093991.html)
- Meal allowance (ND 253 Art. 8): only cash above 1.2 million/person/month is taxable. Meals the employer provides directly (cooked, bought or by voucher) are not taxable. This rule applies only from 01/7/2026. The old 730,000 cap was already abolished by TT 03/2025/TT-BNV. — [LuatVietnam](https://luatvietnam.vn/thue-phi-le-phi/diem-moi-cua-nghi-dinh-253-2026-nd-cp-ve-thue-thu-nhap-ca-nhan-565-110102-article.html); [EasyInvoice](https://easyinvoice.vn/nghi-dinh-253-2026-nd-cp/)
- Housing the employer pays for the employee: taxable at the actual amount, capped at 15% of the employee's other taxable income from that employer. Severance above the legal minimum is exempt if internal rules or the contract provide for it (Art. 8(3)(h)). Pay for untaken annual leave, within the Labour Code Art. 113(3) limits, is exempt (Art. 26). — [LuatVietnam](https://luatvietnam.vn/thue-phi-le-phi/diem-moi-cua-nghi-dinh-253-2026-nd-cp-ve-thue-thu-nhap-ca-nhan-565-110102-article.html)

**10% withholding for casual income**
- ND 253 Art. 50(2) covers payments to residents with no labour contract or a contract under 3 months, including payments to former employees. A payment of 5 million or more per instance needs 10% withholding before payment. Under 5 million, the payer withholds only if the individual asks. Individuals whose annual income after deductions stays below the taxable threshold may give a commitment (cam kết) to avoid withholding. The payer must still report them at year-end. The old threshold under ND 65/2013 was 2 million per payment. — [LuatVietnam](https://luatvietnam.vn/thue-phi-le-phi/diem-moi-cua-nghi-dinh-253-2026-nd-cp-ve-thue-thu-nhap-ca-nhan-565-110102-article.html); [Thuế Phú Thọ via Tạp chí KT-TC](https://thuehaiquan.tapchikinhtetaichinh.vn/huong-dan-khau-tru-10-thue-tncn-voi-khoan-chi-tu-5-trieu-dong-lan-168499.html)
- Residents on contracts of 3 months or more are withheld under the progressive schedule, including those with such contracts at several employers. — [Thuế Phú Thọ via Tạp chí KT-TC](https://thuehaiquan.tapchikinhtetaichinh.vn/huong-dan-khau-tru-10-thue-tncn-voi-khoan-chi-tu-5-trieu-dong-lan-168499.html)
- Some individuals have extra income elsewhere that averages 15 million/month or less and was already withheld at 10%. They need not finalize that extra income. An individual with one employer on a 3-month+ contract may authorise that employer to finalize, even with fewer than 12 months worked. — [VnExpress](https://vnexpress.net/7-diem-moi-nguoi-lao-dong-can-luu-y-khi-tinh-thue-tncn-nam-2026-5093991.html); [EasyInvoice](https://easyinvoice.vn/nghi-dinh-253-2026-nd-cp/)

**Declaration and filing**
- Nghị quyết 66.16/2026/NQ-CP (07/4/2026), Appendix I.9 and Art. 19, switches salary PIT returns from monthly to quarterly. The forms are 02/KK-TNCN and 05/KK-TNCN under TT 80/2021/TT-BTC. The rule runs from 15/4/2026 to the end of 28/02/2027. — [LuatVietnam](https://luatvietnam.vn/tin-van-ban-moi/doanh-nghiep-nop-to-khai-thue-tncn-theo-quy-tu-tu-15-4-2026-den-het-28-02-2027-186-109638-article.html)

If a taxpayer already filed April 2026 monthly, the Q2 return covers only May and June. April's tax is due with the Q2 deadline, 31/7/2026. — [xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn](https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/chuyen-sang-ke-khai-thue-thu-nhap-ca-nhan-theo-quy-tu-ky-thang-4-2026-119260624150148333.htm)

### Inferences
- Monthly quick-calc formula for the 5-bracket schedule. I derived it from Art. 9; no source gives it. T is monthly taxable income in million VND:
  - T ≤ 10: 5%·T
  - 10 < T ≤ 30: 10%·T − 0.5
  - 30 < T ≤ 60: 20%·T − 3.5
  - 60 < T ≤ 100: 30%·T − 9.5
  - T > 100: 35%·T − 14.5
- App date logic: key the PIT engine on payment date (Art. 8(3)), not on the earning month. Any salary paid on or after 01/01/2026 uses the 5-bracket schedule and the 15.5/6.2 deductions. Payments made before then use the 7-bracket schedule and the 11/4.4 deductions.
- Monthly withholding Jan–Jun 2026 may have used the old schedule. Mid-year guidance (ND 253) arrived only on 30/6/2026. The app should support a 2026 year-end recalculation for each employee.
- Casual-income threshold: the 2 million rule applies to payments before 01/7/2026, and 5 million from 01/7/2026 (ND 253 effective date). This boundary is my reading. I found no explicit statement on payments in H1 2026.
- Filing periodicity should be a setting. Quarterly applies 15/4/2026–28/02/2027 under NQ 66.16. After that, the default may revert to monthly or quarterly by VAT period under the Tax Administration rules unless the rule is extended.
- Standard journal entries, based on the TT99 accounts confirmed above. These are textbook entries, not from a fetched source:
  - Withhold salary PIT: Nợ 334 / Có 3335.
  - Casual labour: Nợ 334, or 6422/641/642, / Có 3335.
  - Pay the tax: Nợ 3335 / Có 112.

## Q2. BHXH/BHYT/BHTN/KPCĐ rates, salary floor and cap, minimum wage, accounts

### Takeaway
The 2026 rates for Vietnamese employees are unchanged in percentage terms:
- Employer 21.5%: BHXH 17.5% (14% pension, 3% sickness/maternity, 0.5% occupational accident), BHYT 3%, BHTN 1%. KPCĐ 2% is extra, for 23.5% in total.
- Employee 10.5%: BHXH 8%, BHYT 1.5%, BHTN 1%.

The BHXH/BHYT cap is 20 × mức tham chiếu, where mức tham chiếu equals lương cơ sở. That gives 46.8 million/month until 30/6/2026 (2.34 million base) and 50.6 million from 01/7/2026 (2.53 million base, ND 161/2026). The BHTN cap is 20 × regional minimum wage. The floor is the regional minimum wage under ND 293/2025/NĐ-CP (Region I 5,310,000). ND 399/2026 replaces these minimum wages from 01/01/2027.

### Cited Findings
- Rates for Vietnamese workers in 2026 total 32%. Employer 21.5% = 14% pension + 3% sickness/maternity + 0.5% TNLĐ-BNN + 1% BHTN + 3% BHYT. Employee 10.5% = 8% pension + 1% BHTN + 1.5% BHYT. Basis: Luật BHXH 2024 Art. 32–34, ND 58/2020 Art. 4, QĐ 595/QĐ-BHXH. — [LuatVietnam](https://luatvietnam.vn/bao-hiem/ty-le-dong-bhxh-cua-doanh-nghiep-va-nguoi-lao-dong-563-100379-article.html)
- Foreign workers' total is 30%, because they pay no BHTN. — [LuatVietnam](https://luatvietnam.vn/bao-hiem/ty-le-dong-bhxh-cua-doanh-nghiep-va-nguoi-lao-dong-563-100379-article.html)
- A draft amended BHXH Law (draft dated 21/10/2026) is expected to take effect 01/3/2027. — [LuatVietnam](https://luatvietnam.vn/bao-hiem/ty-le-dong-bhxh-cua-doanh-nghiep-va-nguoi-lao-dong-563-100379-article.html)

Union member dues are 1% of the employee's BHXH salary. — [LuatVietnam](https://luatvietnam.vn/lao-dong-tien-luong/muc-dong-doan-phi-kinh-phi-cong-doan-562-23118-article.html)

### Inferences
- The app needs date-effective parameters:
  - Cap for BHXH/BHYT: 46.8 million before 01/7/2026, then 50.6 million.
  - Cap for BHTN: 20 × the employer's regional minimum wage. For Region I in 2026: 106.2 million. For Region I in 2027: 114 million (source figure).
  - Floor: the regional minimum wage, by year.

  - Payment: Nợ 3382, 3383, 3384, 3386 / Có 112.

## Q3. Lệ phí môn bài: abolished from 01/01/2026?

### Takeaway
Yes. Collection ends from 01/01/2026 under Art. 10(7) of Nghị quyết 198/2025/QH15. Nghị định 362/2025/NĐ-CP (31/12/2025, Art. 6(4)) repealed the môn bài rules, so ND 139/2016 and ND 22/2020 lapsed. No môn bài return or payment is due for 2026 onward. Môn bài still owed for 2025 and earlier years remains collectible. A debt write-off for dormant taxpayers is being drafted.

### Cited Findings
- Công văn hỏa tốc 645/CT-CS (24/1/2026, Cục Thuế) relies on Art. 10(7) of NQ 198/2025/QH15: "Chấm dứt việc thu, nộp lệ phí môn bài từ ngày 1/1/2026". From 01/01/2026, taxpayers pay no môn bài for 2026 and later years and file no môn bài return. This covers enterprises, organisations, household businesses and individuals. — [VnEconomy](https://vneconomy.vn/tu-2026-doanh-nghiep-ho-kinh-doanh-chinh-thuc-khong-phai-khai-va-nop-le-phi-mon-bai.htm)
- Art. 6(4) of ND 362/2025/NĐ-CP (31/12/2025), which guides the Fees and Charges Law, repealed all môn bài provisions. ND 139/2016/NĐ-CP and ND 22/2020/NĐ-CP therefore lapsed. — [VnEconomy](https://vneconomy.vn/tu-2026-doanh-nghiep-ho-kinh-doanh-chinh-thuc-khong-phai-khai-va-nop-le-phi-mon-bai.htm)

### Inferences
- The app should stop auto-generating annual môn bài accruals and the January-30 payment reminder for periods from 01/01/2026.
- The app should keep the ability to record and settle 2025-or-earlier arrears and late interest.
- The "môn bài → 3338" hint is a legacy TT200 practice. I recall TT200 Art. 52 placed môn bài under 3338, but I did not fetch it here. It is a lệ phí by nature. If the app keeps the hint for arrears, 3339 (phí, lệ phí) fits the TT99 names better than 3338/33382 (thuế khác).
- Recommendation for the app: flag the hint as "chỉ dùng cho nợ lệ phí môn bài các năm ≤ 2025" and suggest 3339 under TT99. Leave the user an override for consistency with prior years.

### Gaps

## Q4. Hộ kinh doanh: abolition of thuế khoán (brief)

### Takeaway
From 01/01/2026, the presumptive tax regime (thuế khoán) is abolished. All household and individual businesses must self-declare under NQ 198/2025/QH15 and Nghị định 68/2026/NĐ-CP, as amended by ND 141/2026. The VAT/PIT exemption threshold rose to 1 billion VND revenue per year. Môn bài is also gone. A 30% PIT reduction applies for 2026–2027 when revenue is 10 billion or less (NQ 43/2026/QH16, ND 398/2026).

### Cited Findings
- Thuế khoán was abolished for household and individual businesses from 01/01/2026, and all must self-declare. Môn bài for them ends under Art. 10 of NQ 198/2025/QH15, leaving only VAT and PIT. Revenue up to 1 billion/year is exempt from VAT and PIT (was 500 million). Between 1 and 3 billion, the business may choose PIT on profit or on revenue. Above 3 billion, the profit method is mandatory. — [MISA meInvoice](https://www.meinvoice.vn/tin-tuc/17722/cach-tinh-thue-ho-kinh-doanh/)
- The 1 billion threshold applies from 01/01/2026. — [baochinhphu.vn headline](https://baochinhphu.vn/chinh-thuc-nang-nguong-chiu-thue-voi-ho-kinh-doanh-len-01-ty-dong-nam-ap-dung-tu-1-1-2026-102260429185517215.htm)
- Annual revenue for these thresholds follows ND 68/2026/NĐ-CP as amended by ND 141/2026/NĐ-CP. Individuals with revenue of 10 billion or less get a 30% PIT reduction for 2026 and 2027 under ND 398/2026, declared on Mẫu số 01. — [xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn](https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/giam-thue-thu-nhap-ca-nhan-thue-thu-nhap-doanh-nghiep-119261101192523427.htm)

### Inferences
- This does not affect payroll for a trading company. It matters for supplier-side logic. Household-business suppliers above 1 billion/year must now issue invoices and self-declare, so input documentation from them should improve.

### Gaps
- I did not fetch the full text of ND 68/2026 for exact provisions on the abolition and its declaration periods. The abolition claim rests on a vendor summary and the headline of an official news item.
